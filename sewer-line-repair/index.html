Sewer Backup: What To Do First

For emergency sewer line repair in Maywood, call the 24/7 line. Stop using water in the house, because every flush and shower pushes more wastewater toward the lowest drain. Keep people and pets away from any overflow, and move valuables out of the way.

Sewage is a health hazard, so wash hands after contact and ask the plumber about cleanup. A first step is usually to clear the line so it drains, and a camera run follows to find why it stopped.

Signs The Problem Is The Sewer Line

Several of these together point to the main line rather than a single drain.

  • Two or more fixtures back up at the same time.
  • Gurgling noises in toilets or tubs when another fixture drains.
  • A sewage smell in the yard or near the cleanout.
  • A wet patch or sunken area in the lawn over the line.
  • A drain that clears with a cable and returns within weeks.

Why Sewer Lines Fail

Roots find tiny gaps at joints and grow inside the pipe, narrowing it until it clogs. Older materials such as clay and cast iron crack, corrode or shift. Ground movement and heavy loads above can also crush or offset a line.

The age of the house tells you which pipe is likely. Your county assessor record lists the year it was built, and older homes in a city incorporated in 1924 can have original sections. A camera confirms the actual pipe, which beats guessing.

The line is on your property from the house to the connection with the public sewer, so repairs there are usually the homeowner's job. Ask your city public works department where your responsibility ends.

Code And Rules To Know

Sewer repairs follow the California Plumbing Code (based on the Uniform Plumbing Code, or UPC) for pipe materials, slope and cleanouts, and most cities require a permit and inspection before the trench is closed. Ask who is responsible for the part of the line in the street, since that is often the city.
