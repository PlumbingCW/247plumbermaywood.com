Older Homes, Older Pipe

Houses built many decades ago can still carry original pipe, and the year your house was built sets what is behind the walls. Galvanized steel supply lines corrode from the inside, so low pressure and rusty water at several taps point to the pipe itself, not one fixture. Cast iron and clay drain lines roughen with age and catch grease and roots.
