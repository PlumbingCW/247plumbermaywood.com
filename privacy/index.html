What We Collect

This website has no forms, so we do not collect names, addresses or messages through it. When you call (213) 672-5590, we receive your phone number and whatever you tell us about the job.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call and we will note it.

How Information Is Used

We use call details to send a plumber, to price the work, to follow up on the visit and to improve how we answer calls. We do not sell the information you give us.

Plumbers who are sent to your address receive the details they need to do the job, such as your address and a description of the problem.

Website Data

Like most sites, the hosting service may record technical data such as pages viewed, browser type and approximate location. We use it to keep the site working and to see which pages are useful.

You can limit tracking through your browser settings. Blocking it will not stop you from using any part of the site.

Your Choices

You can ask what we hold about your calls, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where the law allows. Email contact@247plumbermaywood.com with your phone number and the date of the call.
