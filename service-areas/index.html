Where Dispatch Reaches

Maywood sits in the middle of a tight cluster of Los Angeles County cities, and a plumber dispatched from Slauson Avenue reaches the four towns below faster than most companies reach them from outside the area. Each town has its own page with local notes, ZIP codes and the plumbing problems that show up most there.

We also take calls from Cudahy, Bell Gardens and Walnut Park, which sit between the towns above. Those places do not have separate pages because the plumbing advice matches the nearest town page, so read Bell for Cudahy and Bell Gardens and Huntington Park for Walnut Park.

Bell lies directly south of Maywood and shares Atlantic Boulevard with it. Calls from Bell are mostly older homes and small multi-unit buildings, so slow drains, aging supply pipe and water heater failures lead. Read the Bell page for the pipe-age notes and the shared-drain advice.

Huntington Park is the dense commercial city to the southwest. Shops and restaurants along Pacific Boulevard call about grease and floor drains, while homes on the side streets call about leaks and old pipe. The city's 2025 water report lists hard water, which the Huntington Park page explains with the numbers.

Commerce, to the east, is an industrial and commercial city with warehouses, shops and a few residential blocks. Calls there often involve floor drains, grease interceptors and backflow devices, and the Commerce page covers who approves repairs in leased buildings and what the Cal Water report says about hardness.

South Gate, to the southeast, grew from a 1917 subdivision into a city of homes and factories, and its 2025 water report puts hardness at about 14 grains per gallon by our conversion. The South Gate page explains what that means for water heaters and older houses.

A few practical notes help whichever town you are in. Tell dispatch whether you own or rent, because a landlord may need to approve the work. Say whether the building is a house, a duplex or a larger property, since shared lines change the diagnosis. Mention the age of the house if you know it, and whether the water heater, the supply pipe or the drains have been replaced before.

These details take a minute and often save a second visit.

Our service area pages and guides use public sources such as city water reports and state licensing rules, and where we could not confirm a local figure we say so. If you live in one of the towns above and your water supplier differs from the one named on the page, trust your own bill and report over our summary, and tell us so we can update the page.

Each town is served from the same dispatch point, so there is no travel zone surcharge to explain on the phone. ZIP codes on the town pages are for orientation only. Bell is mainly 90201, Huntington Park 90255, Commerce 90040 and South Gate 90280, and Maywood itself is 90270.

Several of these ZIP codes are shared with neighboring places, so if you are unsure, give dispatch your street and cross street instead of a ZIP code and we will confirm range by address.

Plumbing in every one of these towns follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and permits come from each city's building department. What happens when you call is the same in every town. Dispatch asks for your address, the nearest cross streets and what you see, then tells you what to shut off while a plumber is sent from Slauson Avenue.

Same-day appointments are offered when available. We do not promise a fixed arrival time, because traffic and open calls change by the hour. You hear a clear price before work starts.

Florence-Graham lies west of Huntington Park and is covered the same way. If your address is close to one of these edges, call and give the cross streets so dispatch can confirm range.
