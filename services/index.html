How Our Plumbing Pages Work

Every page below covers one kind of plumbing problem: the signs, what we check, the repair choices and what moves the price. Start with the symptom you see, since that is usually how a problem first shows up.

Urgent problems such as a burst pipe, a sewer backup or a dead water heater are marked with emergency wording because the line is answered around the clock. Planned jobs such as an install, a repipe or a softener are scheduled and priced before work starts.

Work on every page follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and each page names the rules that matter for that job. Some services do not need their own page. Backflow testing, grease trap cleaning, storm drain cleaning, cast iron pipe repair and hybrid or gas water heater work are covered as sections on the pages for plumbing installation, commercial plumbing, sewer line repair and water heater repair.
