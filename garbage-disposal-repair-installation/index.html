Disposal Leaking Or Jammed Right Now

If you need emergency garbage disposal repair in Maywood, call the 24/7 line. Switch the disposal off at the wall and unplug it or flip its breaker before anyone reaches near it. Never put a hand into the disposal, even when it is off.

A leak under the sink can soak cabinets quickly. Put a bucket under the leak, close the sink supply valves if water is coming from a pipe and call. A disposal that hums but does not spin is jammed, and a plumber can free it safely.

Reattaching Water Lines And Dishwashers

A disposal is connected to the dishwasher and to the sink drain, and both connections can loosen or leak. A dishwasher that drains slowly or puts water back into the sink often has a clogged drain hose or a blocked disposal inlet.

We reconnect the dishwasher line and the drain properly when we install or replace a disposal. If the dishwasher is new, tell the dispatcher so the plumber brings the right fittings.

Installing A New Disposal

Choosing a unit

Horsepower, feed type and noise level differ. A larger motor handles tougher scraps, and continuous feed is common. A plumber matches a unit to the sink and the power available.

What installation involves

The plumber removes the old unit, sets the mounting, connects the drain and dishwasher line, and tests for leaks and power. Ask about a new electrical cord if the old one is worn. See plumbing installation if the sink is being moved.

Living With A Disposal

Good habits keep the unit and the drain working.

  • Run cold water before, during and after use.
  • Keep grease, bones, fibrous scraps and coffee grounds out.
  • Never put chemical cleaners into a disposal.
  • Run a few ice cubes now and then to clean the blades.
  • If it jams, switch it off and call, since resets do not clear the cause.

Code And Rules To Know

A disposal connects to the drain trap, which the California Plumbing Code (based on the Uniform Plumbing Code, or UPC) regulates, and to electrical wiring that falls under electrical code. A plumber can swap a unit on an existing outlet, but new wiring needs a licensed electrician.
