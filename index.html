Local Notes

Plumbing In Maywood, From Slauson To Atlantic

Maywood is a compact city of about 23,600 people inside Los Angeles County, incorporated in 1924. Slauson Avenue runs east to west through the middle of town and Atlantic Boulevard runs north to south toward I-710. Our dispatch address is on Slauson Avenue, so a plumber heading out reaches most blocks along one of those two corridors.

Houses in a city this age were built in different decades, and the decade decides the pipes. Older homes can still carry galvanized steel supply lines or cast iron drains, while later repairs added copper, PVC or PEX. Your county assessor record lists the year your house was built, and that year tells a plumber what to expect behind the wall before the first call.

Neighbors share more than a street here. Families who use Maywood Park on East 58th Street, Maywood Riverfront Park on Slauson Avenue or Pixley Park on 56th Street also share the same wet winters and the same small lots. They also share a habit of calling a plumber after a drain slows down rather than before. That pattern is why drain cleaning and water heater work make up most of our calls.

Shopping at Maywood Towne Center near Slauson and Atlantic often means a kitchen full of groceries and a garbage disposal that has been running for years. A disposal that hums but does not spin, or a sink that drains slowly after a big meal, is a repair we can usually finish in one visit. The garbage disposal repair page explains what we check first.

Water comes from local suppliers, and many Maywood addresses are served by one of the Maywood Mutual Water companies. Your bill names your supplier. We do not quote a hardness figure for Maywood because we have not found a published one for these suppliers. Ask your supplier for its annual water quality report, or read about water softener and filtration options before you buy anything.
