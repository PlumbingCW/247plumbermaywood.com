Who You Reach When You Call

Maywood 24/7 Plumbing is a dispatch line based at 4410 Slauson Avenue in Maywood, California. When you call, you reach someone who asks what is happening, tells you what to shut off, and sends a licensed and insured plumber to your address.

We cover Maywood and the neighboring towns of Bell, Huntington Park, Commerce and South Gate. The line is open all day and all night because plumbing problems do not follow a work schedule.

How A Job Works

The plumber diagnoses the problem first and then tells you what the repair involves and what it costs. You hear a clear price before work starts, and the work begins only after you agree to it.

Some problems have two reasonable fixes. A failing water heater might be repaired or replaced, and a slow main drain might need cleaning now and a camera look later. Your plumber explains the trade-offs and the order of cost so you can decide.

What We Will Not Promise

We do not promise an exact arrival time, because traffic on Atlantic Boulevard and the I-710 corridor changes by the hour. We offer same-day appointments when available and we tell you plainly when a visit has to wait.

We also do not quote a final price over the phone for work nobody has seen. A phone number can give you the factors that drive cost, and the plumber turns that into a real figure on site.

Where Our Calls Come From

Most calls come from the five cities nearest the Slauson Avenue base: Maywood, Bell, Huntington Park, Commerce and South Gate. Homes in these cities range from older single-family houses to small apartment buildings, and the plumbing problems follow the age of the buildings. Old supply pipe, aging drains and water heaters worn down by hard groundwater lead the list.

City water reports for Huntington Park, South Gate and Commerce list hardness in the moderate to hard range, so scale is a regular topic in our heater and fixture work. For your own address we suggest testing your tap and asking your supplier for its latest report, since we do not publish a figure for Maywood that we have not confirmed.

How We Write And Check Our Guides

The guides and service pages on this site are written by our staff using public sources, such as city water reports, state licensing rules and the building code. Where we use a local figure we say where it comes from and the year. Where we could not confirm a number, we say so and tell you how to check it yourself.

We do not post reviews or star ratings on this site because we do not want to show numbers we cannot back. A plumber's license, a written price and a clear warranty are better signs than a score, and our license-check guide shows how to verify them. If you spot an error on any page, call the number in the footer and tell the dispatcher, and we will correct it.

How To Check Any Plumber Before You Hire

California requires a contractor license for most plumbing work, and the plumbing classification is C-36. The Contractors State License Board lets anyone look up a license at cslb.ca.gov and see whether it is current, which classification it carries and whether a bond is on file.

Ask for the license number before a visit, look it up yourself, and match the name on the record to the name on the truck and the paperwork. That check takes two minutes and applies to us and to every other company you call.

Our Standards And Safety Notes

California plumbing work is governed by the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) and is part of Title 24 of the California Code of Regulations. Permit rules differ by city, so ask the building department in your city whether a repair needs a permit.

For gas smells, leave the building first and call the gas utility or 911. A plumber is the right call after the area is safe, not before.
