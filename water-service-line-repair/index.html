Water Line Leak: What To Do First

For emergency water line repair in Maywood, call the 24/7 line if water is rising through the lawn or driveway, if there is no water at all or if the meter runs with the house valve closed. Close the house valve at the front of the house and note whether the meter keeps moving.

If the meter keeps moving with the house valve closed, the leak is between the meter and the house, which is the service line. If the water supplier owns the line up to the meter, tell the dispatcher and the plumber will explain who is responsible for which part.

Where The Responsibility Changes

A service line has two parts. The public side runs from the main in the street to the meter and is usually the supplier's responsibility. The private side runs from the meter to the house and is usually yours. The exact point varies by supplier, so ask yours where it sits.

Many Maywood addresses buy water from one of the Maywood Mutual Water companies, and others are served by neighboring systems. Your bill names your supplier, and the supplier can tell you what it maintains. Call the supplier if the leak looks like it is on its side.

Signs Of A Service Line Problem

Any of these is worth a call.

  • Wet or sunken ground between the meter and the house.
  • A meter that runs with the house valve closed.
  • Low pressure at every fixture at once.
  • A sudden jump in the water bill.
  • Dirty water after a repair or a main break.
  • Water pooling in the yard with no rain or sprinklers.

Repair Or Replace The Line

Spot repair

A localized leak can be repaired by digging to the break and replacing a short section. It works when the rest of the pipe is in good shape.

Replacement

A line with repeated leaks or old, corroded pipe is better replaced. Trenchless methods can replace some lines without trenching a whole yard, depending on the layout.

Buried Line Leaks And How They Are Found

Buried leaks are found with a pressure test and acoustic tools that listen along the line. The plumber marks the spot so a small hole is dug rather than a long trench. See leak detection for how those tools work.

Before any digging, the plumber should check where other utilities run. Calling 811 before digging is standard practice in California, and the plumber will tell you how that is handled for your job.

Code And Rules To Know

The line between the meter and the house is usually the owner's responsibility, and the line up to the meter belongs to the supplier. Repairs follow the California Plumbing Code (based on the Uniform Plumbing Code, or UPC) and often need a permit. Call your supplier to confirm where the boundary is.
