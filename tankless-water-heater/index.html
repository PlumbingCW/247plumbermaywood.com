How A Tankless Water Heater Differs From A Tank

A tankless water heater in Maywood heats water only when a tap opens, so there is no tank to leak, no standby heat loss and no running out of hot water in a long shower. The price for that is a larger gas line or a heavy electric supply, proper venting and regular care.

The practical limit is flow. A tankless unit has a maximum flow rate, and two showers plus a dishwasher can exceed it. Your plumber looks at how many fixtures run at once and sizes the unit accordingly.

Because tankless units are compact, they are often mounted on an exterior or garage wall. Ask where the vent terminates and where condensate drains, since both affect where the unit can go.

Flushing And Descaling

Flush and descale is the most common tankless service we hear about. Mineral scale builds up inside the heat exchanger, cuts flow, causes error codes and shortens the unit's life. A flush circulates a mild acid solution through the unit to dissolve scale, then rinses it clean.

How often depends on your water. Hardness reported by nearby suppliers is moderate to high, including 146 ppm average for City of Commerce groundwater and 238.2 ppm for South Gate in its 2025 report. We have not found a published figure for the Maywood Mutual Water companies, so ask your supplier for its water quality report. A softener can reduce scale at the source.

If your tankless heater was never flushed and is several years old, say so on the call. The plumber may recommend a flush before any repair, since scale causes several error codes.

What To Check Before You Call

A few quick checks can save a service call.

  • Is the gas valve open and the power switched on?
  • Does the display show an error code, and what is it?
  • Is the vent free of obstructions such as nests, snow or debris?
  • Do you have hot water at one fixture but not another?
  • When was the unit last flushed?

Installing A New Tankless Unit

A tankless install often needs more than a swap. The gas line may need upsizing, the vent may need to be rerouted, and an electric model may need a bigger circuit. The plumber checks all three before quoting.

A planned install is covered in more detail on the water heater installation page. We serve Maywood ZIP code 90270, and nearby 90201, 90255, 90040 and 90280, and the process is the same in each.

Code And Rules To Know

Tankless units need correct venting and gas line sizing, and both are set by the manufacturer and the California Plumbing Code (based on the Uniform Plumbing Code, or UPC). A permit is normally required, and the inspector checks the gas line and vent. Ask for the final sign-off before you pay the balance.
