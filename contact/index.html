Call First, Always

The fastest way to reach a plumber is the phone. Dial (213) 672-5590, tell the dispatcher what is happening and where, and follow the shutoff steps you are given while a plumber is sent.

We do not use contact forms. Forms add a delay that a leaking pipe cannot afford, and a call lets us ask the two or three questions that decide which plumber to send.

What To Have Ready

A few details make the call shorter. Know your street address and the nearest cross street, which fixture or room is affected, whether the water is clean or dirty, and whether you have already closed the main valve.

If you rent, tell us. We can work with property managers and landlords, and we will ask who approves the repair.

Where We Are

Our address is 4410 Slauson Avenue, Maywood, CA 90270. Slauson Avenue is the main east to west street through the city, close to Atlantic Boulevard and the Maywood Towne Center.

This is a dispatch address, not a walk-in showroom. Plumbers travel to you, so call before visiting the address.

Email

For non-urgent questions, invoices or documents, write to contact@247plumbermaywood.com. Do not email an emergency, because nobody can promise to read it in time.
