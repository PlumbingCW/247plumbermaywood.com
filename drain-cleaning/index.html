When A Clogged Drain Is An Emergency

If you need emergency drain cleaning in Maywood, call us now. The line is open 24/7, and the plumber we send can usually start with a cable machine the same visit. Stop running water in the house, because every sink, shower and washer you use pushes more water toward the clog.

Call right away if wastewater is coming up in a tub or floor drain, if more than one fixture is slow at once, or if a kitchen sink is full and not draining at all. Those signs point to a blockage in a branch or main line, and waiting makes the mess bigger.

What Usually Clogs A Maywood Drain

Most clogs we clear fall into four groups. Kitchen drains clog with cooking grease that cools and hardens inside the pipe. Bathroom drains clog with hair and soap scum. Toilets and main lines clog with wipes, paper towels and flushable products that are not truly flushable. Older lines clog where tree roots find a joint.

The age of your pipes matters. A house built many decades ago may still have cast iron or clay sections buried between the house and the street, and rough pipe walls catch debris faster than smooth modern PVC or ABS. Your county assessor record shows the year your home was built, which tells your plumber what to expect.

Shared walls and shared lines change the picture. If you live in a duplex or a small building, a clog that shows up in your unit may start in a line you share, so tell the dispatcher how many units use the same drain.

Kitchen Sink Clogs

Why the kitchen sink backs up first

Kitchen lines carry grease, food solids and soap, and the pipe under the sink is the narrowest and most bent part of the run. A slow sink after big meals usually means grease has narrowed the pipe, not that the line is broken.

What we do about it

A plumber removes the trap, runs a small cable through the branch line and flushes it with hot water. If a garbage disposal sits in front of the clog, we check that too, which is why the garbage disposal repair page is worth a look if yours hums or jams.

If a drain clogs again within weeks, the cable probably opened a hole and left the cause behind. That is the time to jet the line or look at it with a camera.

What To Do While You Wait

These steps keep a bad clog from getting worse before the plumber arrives.

  • Stop using every sink, shower, toilet and washer in the house.
  • Do not pour chemical drain cleaner into a drain that is completely stopped, because it can sit in the pipe and burn the plumber or the pipe.
  • Move rugs, boxes and electronics away from low floor drains and tub areas.
  • Locate your main cleanout, often a capped pipe outside near the foundation, and tell the dispatcher where it is.
  • If sewage is overflowing, keep children and pets away and wash hands after any contact.

Drain Line Leaks And Main Line Clogs

A drain that smells, gurgles or leaves a wet patch under a floor can be leaking rather than clogged. Water that slowly seeps from a broken drain line rots framing and draws pests, and cabling the line will not fix it. If your plumber suspects a leak, the next step is a camera run through the pipe.

When the main line backs up, every drain downstream of the clog shows it, usually the lowest tub or floor drain first. A main line clog is a sewer problem, and you will find the details on the sewer line cleaning page.

Code And Rules To Know

Drain work follows the California Plumbing Code (based on the Uniform Plumbing Code, or UPC), part of Title 24 of the state building standards, which covers traps, vents and cleanouts. Local building departments enforce it, so a repair that changes the drain layout may need a permit. Ask the plumber whether yours does.
