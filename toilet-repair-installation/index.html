Overflowing Or Leaking Toilet: Act Now

If you need emergency toilet repair in Maywood, call the 24/7 line. The first step is to stop the water. Find the valve on the wall or floor behind the toilet and turn it clockwise, or lift the tank lid and hold the float up so no more water enters.

Do not flush again if the bowl is rising. Put towels around the base, move rugs and call. A toilet that leaks from the base is a sealing problem, and a bowl that keeps rising points to a clog or a drain issue.

Running Toilet And Leaks At The Base

A running toilet wastes water

A toilet that hisses or refills on its own is leaking from the tank to the bowl. The wasted water shows up in your bill. A new flapper or fill valve usually solves it.

A leak at the base is not a small problem

Water at the base means the seal is failing, and the wax ring or the flange beneath is the likely cause. Left alone, water can rot the subfloor. A plumber pulls the toilet, checks the flange, replaces the ring and sets the toilet level.

Replacing Or Installing A Toilet

A new toilet is worth it when the porcelain is cracked, when repairs repeat or when you want a more efficient model. A plumber measures the rough-in distance from the wall to the drain, which decides which toilets will fit, and checks the flange and the shutoff valve before setting the new one.

Installation includes setting the toilet on a new seal, connecting the supply, adjusting the fill and testing for leaks. Ask whether the old toilet will be removed. If you are remodeling, the plumbing installation page covers moving or adding fixtures.

A faulty angle stop or supply line behind the toilet is a frequent hidden problem. Replacing it at the same visit costs little and avoids a second call.

What To Do While You Wait

These steps limit damage while the plumber is on the way.

  • Close the shutoff valve behind the toilet.
  • Lay towels around the base and keep people off the wet floor.
  • Do not use the toilet until it is checked.
  • Remove the tank lid and look for a sticking float or a cracked tank if water is leaking.
  • Use another bathroom if you have one.

Local Water And Toilet Parts

Hard water leaves scale on flush valves and fill valves, and that scale is a common cause of a toilet that runs on. Nearby suppliers report hardness well above soft water, including 238.2 ppm for South Gate in its 2025 report. We have not found a figure for the Maywood Mutual Water companies, so ask your supplier for its annual report.

Code And Rules To Know

California limits flush volume on new toilets, so a replacement uses less water than an older one, and the California Plumbing Code (based on the Uniform Plumbing Code, or UPC) governs the connection to the drain. A new toilet in a remodeled bathroom may need a permit, so ask the building department.
